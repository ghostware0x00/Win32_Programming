JOB ROLE (SECURITY RESEARCHER) -> COMPANY (McAfee)

- After you study stuff related to the job, make projects which solve that issue and add it in resume

Responsible for malware research, false positive analysis, test set development, and execution of EPP/ATP efficacy tests to ensure accurate assessment of product protection against real-world threats and alignment with third-party testing methodologies.

This is a Hybrid position located at our Bangalore, India development center. You will be required to be on-site 2 to 3 days per week. When you are not working on-site, you will be working from your home office. We are only considering candidates within a commutable distance to our Bangalore office and are not offering relocation assistance at this time.

Position Details

About The Role

    Analyze prevalent malware samples and validate malicious behaviour and detection effectiveness.
    Investigate false positives and detection gaps, providing root-cause analysis and recommendations.
    Own EPP/ATP efficacy test planning, execution, maintenance, and reporting. Develop attack simulations, curate behaviorally active malware test sets, and expand test coverage.
    Align testing with third-party methodologies and collaborate with Engineering, Research and Data teams to improve detection quality.

About Me

    We are looking for 2 to 3 years strong expertise in malware analysis, threat intelligence and endpoint security technologies, including EPP, EDR, XDR, and ATP.
    I am experienced with VirusTotal, Hybrid Analysis, ANY.RUN, Joe Sandbox, CyberChef, MITRE ATT&CK, Procmon, Process Explorer, Wireshark, Sysmon and Windows Event Analysis.
    I use threat intelligence sources, reputation systems, Python, and PowerShell to investigate threats, automate analysis, and improve efficiency.
    I have strong analytical, troubleshooting, and reporting skills, with experience supporting third-party efficacy testing such as AV-Comparatives, AV-TEST, SE Labs and MRG Effitas.
    I have knowledge of malware reverse engineering and attack simulation techniques and use AI/LLM tools to accelerate threat analysis and efficacy investigations.

WHAT TO KNOW THEN

Malware Analysis & Triage (Dynamic & Static)

    Dynamic behavior monitoring: Tracing process trees, injections, persistence mechanisms (Registry run keys, Scheduled Tasks, WMI), and file/network activity using Procmon, Process Explorer, Wireshark, and Sysmon.

    Command-Line analysis & obfuscation: Decoding heavily obfuscated PowerShell, VBScript, Command Prompt scripts, and macro-based payloads using CyberChef and Regex.

    Static analysis fundamentals: Inspecting PE headers (sections, import/export tables, compile timestamps, rich headers), entropy scoring for packed/encrypted binaries, and hashing mechanics (MD5, SHA256, ssdeep/fuzzy hashing, imphash).

    Sandbox analysis: Interpreting execution logs, API call sequences, and behavior scores from platforms like ANY.RUN, Hybrid Analysis, Joe Sandbox, and VirusTotal.

False Positive (FP) & Detection Gap Analysis

    Root-cause investigation: Differentiating benign administrative behavior (e.g., PSExec, legitimate PowerShell scripts, IT management tools) from living-off-the-land techniques (LotL/LOLBins).

    Tuning and recommendations: Formulating precise exclusion rules, signature updates, or behavioral rule modifications to eliminate FPs without opening security blind spots.

    Telemetry parsing: Navigating Windows Event Logs (Event IDs 4688, 4624, 7045, etc.) and endpoint sensor telemetry to pinpoint why a rule failed to trigger or triggered erroneously.

EPP / EDR / XDR / ATP Efficacy Testing & Test Set Curation

    Test set curation: Building and maintaining fresh, behaviorally active malware sets (ransomware, infostealers, loaders, RATs) while filtering out dead/inactive C2 samples or corrupted binaries.

    Attack simulation: Executing controlled adversary techniques using frameworks like Atomic Red Team, custom PowerShell/Python scripts, or Caldera to test protection and detection layers.

    Test environment isolation: Setting up, managing, and reverting nested virtualization lab environments (Hyper-V, VMware ESXi, VirtualBox) with proper network isolation/faking (INetSim, FakeNet-NG) to prevent accidental spillover.

Third-Party Efficacy Testing Methodologies

    Standard methodologies: Understanding how external testing houses (AV-Comparatives, AV-TEST, SE Labs, MRG Effitas) structure their Real-World Protection, Malware Protection, and False Alarm tests.

    Metrics & Scoring: Familiarity with metrics like Protection Rate, Detection Rate, FP Count, Compromise vs. Block points, and Time-to-Detect/Time-to-Protect.

    Competitive benchmarking: Replicating third-party test workflows internally to catch detection gaps before official public evaluation runs.

MITRE ATT&CK Mapping & Threat Intelligence

    ATT&CK Framework: Mapping observed malware behaviors, attack vectors, and simulation artifacts to specific ATT&CK Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures (TTPs).

    Threat Intel Integration: Leveraging IOCs, YARA rules, STIX/TAXII feeds, and reputation engines to evaluate threat prevalence and prioritize test set additions.

Automation & Analysis Acceleration

    Scripting: Writing Python and PowerShell scripts to automate sample downloading, hash lookup against VT APIs, sample renaming, PE metadata extraction, and report generation.

    AI / LLM utilization: Using LLM prompts effectively to explain decompiled code snippets, translate assembly blocks, generate regex strings, or draft post-incident root-cause analysis summaries.

MALWARE RESEARCH ENGINEER (MAC)


We're looking for a Mac Malware Researcher who will own end-to-end analysis of sophisticated macOS threats—from reverse engineering Mach-O binaries to developing detection strategies that ship directly into our security products. You'll sit at the intersection of threat research and engineering, collaborating closely with detection and product teams to harden coverage for 25 million+ users. This role is open as we accelerate our macOS security capabilities and need a technically sharp researcher who can both dig deep into malware internals and surface findings externally through blogs, whitepapers, and industry conferences.

What You'll Do

    Reverse engineer Mach-O binaries, malicious installers (PKG, DMG), and scripts to identify spyware, backdoors, loaders, and adware behaviors
    Develop and maintain detection signatures, behavioral rules, YARA rules, and generic/heuristic signatures to deliver rapid, scalable protection
    Research macOS-specific persistence mechanisms—LaunchAgents, LaunchDaemons, cron jobs, login items—and study evasion techniques including notarization abuse, code signing misuse, and sandbox bypass attempts
    Conduct advanced static and dynamic analysis, memory forensics, and behavioral analysis to track malware families, variants, and evolving attack techniques including zero-days
    Write technical reports and threat intelligence documentation, and advocate for research findings through internal stakeholder briefings, external blogs, whitepapers, and industry conferences
    Partner with detection engineering and product teams to translate research insights into measurable improvements in macOS security coverage

What We're Looking For

    3–5 years of hands-on Mac malware analysis experience with demonstrated proficiency in reverse engineering tools including Hopper, IDA Pro, Ghidra, and LLDB
    Deep knowledge of macOS internals and security architecture—Gatekeeper, XProtect, SIP, TCC—and the Mach-O file format and DYLD dynamic library loading
    Proficiency in C, C++, Objective-C, and Swift, with working knowledge of shell scripting (bash, zsh) and Python
    Proven experience creating YARA rules and generic/heuristic signatures with measurable detection outcomes
    Strong understanding of networking, C2 communication patterns, and persistence techniques specific to macOS
